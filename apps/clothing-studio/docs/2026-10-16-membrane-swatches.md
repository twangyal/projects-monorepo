## Model and units

The material-basis energy per rest area is `0.5*(C11*e1² + 2*C12*e1*e2 + C22*e2² + G12*gamma12²)`. Strains are dimensionless; engineering gamma12 is twice tensor epsilon12. The four coefficients and stress resultants are **N/m**, not Pa or N/m². No thickness is multiplied in again. Areal density is kg/m², rest coordinates/displacements metres, forces N and integrated energy J.

At grain angle theta, c=cos(theta),s=sin(theta), the global engineering strain [exx,eyy,gamma_xy] maps to [c² exx+s² eyy+cs gamma_xy, s² exx+c² eyy−cs gamma_xy, −2cs exx+2cs eyy+(c²−s²)gamma_xy]. Stress resultants transform with the transpose of this map, preserving virtual work. This is a linear infinitesimal-strain law in fixed flat material coordinates, independent of visible print orientation. It is not valid for finite rotations or wrinkled/compressive cloth.

Illustrative compliant: density.18,C11=200,C22=100,C12=20,G12=40. Illustrative firm: density.32,C11=1200,C22=600,C12=60,G12=200. All coefficients are repository-authored demonstrations, not measured fabric specimens. Positive density/C11/C22/G12 and C11*C22>C12² are required; coupling is independent of shear and density. The current fabric library and garment solver do not use these values.

The rectangular grid has two constant-strain triangles per cell (32 or128 total). For each triangle derive the linear shape gradients from original metre coordinates; assemble `area*B^T*C_global*B`. Apply total force F as uniform x-directed right-edge traction, with half nodal weights at the two endpoints. Fix x on the left edge and y only at its lower corner. The left edge is **not fully clamped**: it can contract vertically, with other edges traction-free. Solve the reduced system (at most152 free DOFs) using partial-pivot elimination. Recompute free-force residuals and signed total support forces from the original stiffness matrix. Element strain/stress/area, complete nodes/triangles, profile provenance, assumptions and units remain in JSON.

For uniform stress Nx=F/W at0°, determinant d=C11*C22−C12² gives exx=Nx*C22/d,eyy=−Nx*C12/d,gamma=0. At90°swap C11/C22. At45°, let S11=C22/d,S22=C11/d,S12=−C12/d: exx=Nx*((S11+S22+2S12)/4+1/(4G12)); eyy has the negative shear-compliance term; gamma=Nx*(S11−S22)/2. Displacements ux=exx*x,uy=gamma*x+eyy*y satisfy this support convention; U=F*extension/2.
