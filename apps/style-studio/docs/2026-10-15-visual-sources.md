# Visual source decision — 2026-10-15 (#264)

The guided flow ships twelve original SVG outfit illustrations authored in this repository. They show recognizable tops, trousers and shoes with declared palette, silhouette/style details and formality; they are examples, not photographs, owned clothing, commercial listings or automatic garment recognition. No third-party image rights or account are needed for these authored assets. Existing local reference-photo imports remain available; users supply their own references and tags.

## Pinterest assessment

Checked official sources on 2026-10-15:

- [Connect an app](https://developer.pinterest.com/docs/getting-started/connect-app/): business account, verified email, developer terms, app registration and approved trial access precede app credentials; OAuth requires a configured redirect.
- [Access tiers](https://dev.pinterest.com/docs/key-concepts/access-tiers/) and [rate limits](https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/reference/rate-limits/): access and quotas require explicit review; a trial quota does not establish a suitable permanent free integration.
- [Developer terms and guidelines](https://developers.pinterest.com/terms/): approval, privacy/consent, content-use and retention constraints need to be reconciled with any persistent offline reference or preference-training design. Public visibility is not a reusable image license.

No approved app/token was supplied for this work. Pricing, overage behavior and rights for persistent copies/model use were not established; no app registration, credential collection, account connection, scrape, purchase or Pinterest integration was performed. These dependencies do not block the original, offline catalog. A later optional integration must verify approved scopes, permitted retention/uses, user consent/deletion, attribution, quotas and no-charge behavior before selection. This is an implementation decision based on the documented requirements, not legal advice or an assertion of API acceptance.
