# Measured skirt construction

The owner’s revised CLO-level vision requires physical patterns linked to 3D construction. Existing tee illustration and photo overlay are retained unchanged. First milestone #166 adds a two-panel developable flared-skirt pattern; a following milestone assembles the exact measurements into a rigid 3D preview. Neither establishes cloth simulation, body fitting or sewing acceptance.

Each panel is one half of a conical-frustum net, an annular sector. Inputs: waist circumference 50–150cm, hem circumference 70–300cm, slant seam length 20–120cm; hem must exceed waist by at least 5cm, and radial increase (hem-waist)/(2π) must be strictly less than slant length. Defaults 80/140/60cm. Inner net radius = slant*waist/(hem-waist); outer radius = inner+slant; panel angle = (hem-waist)/(2*slant). This gives two matching panels with waist arc waist/2, hem arc hem/2 and equal slant-length side seams. Surface height in assembly is sqrt(slant²-radialIncrease²). No eased curves, seam allowance, closures, waistband, hems or anatomical fit are implied.
