# Matched garment fabric comparison — #270

In **Body & drape**, open **Compare fabrics on this garment**, choose two different library fabrics and **Compare garment fabrics**. Start from an existing top and apply/discard any raw product/body edits first. Both candidates use the same committed cut, entered body, mesh and default 240-frame/10-substep garment solver. Non-rib parts take the candidate fabric; rib trims, colors, prints, artwork and saved colorways retain their original data.

The two cards show actual garment-only front/back PNGs from the solved positions, with the same fixed camera and lighting. These views do not follow the interactive orbit camera. Diagnostics report structural-edge extension, maximum final speed, minimum body clearance and contact share. Excessive extension (>100%), residual motion (>10 cm/s) and clearance below 0.34 cm are flagged as numerical concerns. These engineering guards are not calibrated material yield, convergence tolerances or fit acceptance.

Comparing leaves the active design unchanged. **Use [fabric]** changes non-rib materials as one normal undoable edit, preserving styling and artwork; it does not restyle saved colorways. Raw product/body edits block both Compare and Use. Changing the actual garment/body or comparison choices clears the old results. Passive tool navigation retains them. Cancel and leaving the page retire the owned worker and render generation. Old successes and failures cannot replace or erase a new result; workers are terminated and snapshot scenes are disposed through the existing export API. Failed work can be retried explicitly.

Independent comparison controls preserve pending artwork decoding and backup-import authority. An explicit Use is an editing intent and retires those older operations, just like other garment edits. Results are session-only. The local JSON download is a comparison receipt, not an importable project backup: it includes the original garment/body, both candidate products, library fabric identity/GSM, all actual draped positions, body and metrics, fixed settings, units and limitations. It can include your source artwork and is larger than a normal single-project backup; it is not uploaded anywhere.

The existing material/contact model remains approximate: texture-based isotropic stretch/bend, library weight, assumed body anatomy and clearance/friction, carried pockets and no cloth self/layer collision. Independent tensile/bending/membrane swatch laws are not integrated. Comparing two images or finite numerical results does not validate a physical fabric specimen, garment fit or manufacturing readiness. Clothing remains ACTIVE.

Verification and retained first failures are recorded in the root integration receipt. The tests use actual garment-worker replies and snapshot rendering; controlled holds/faults exercise ownership at asynchronous browser boundaries, not physical inputs or measured-reference acceptance.
