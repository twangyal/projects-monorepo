# Fabric drape simulation — issue #219

## Intended outcome and gap

The direction asks for credible cloth simulation, where fabric properties affect drape and folds on a personalized body. The measured skirt was only a rigid cone, with no material behaviour or body contact.

## Design (`src/drape.ts`)

- **Mesh from the patterns.** Each panel's annular sector is sampled as 25 radial rows × 49 angular columns. The two panels share their side-seam columns, giving 96 columns around the skirt.
  - Rest lengths are flat-pattern distances. Constraints that cross a side seam continue the unfolded sector, because the seam joins identical edges.
  - The assembled cone is therefore the unstretched starting state; measured worst initial strain is below 0.2%, from chord discretization.
- **XPBD solver.** Gravity is 981 cm/s². The solver runs 240 frames of 1/60 s with 12 substeps, applies mild velocity damping, and uses particle masses from each cell's flat area times the fabric weight.
- **Constraints.**
  - Warp and weft distance constraints, with stretch compliance per fabric.
  - Shear diagonals.
  - Two-segment bending distances, with compliance set by fabric stiffness.
  - Long-range attachments: no point may be farther from its waist anchor than the warp length allows, plus 0.5%.
- **Waist and body.** The waist row is fixed; fabric cannot rise above the waistline. The body is a surface of revolution (waist, linear to the hip, then constant), and the fabric is pushed out to a 4 mm offset.
- **Symmetry breaking.** A seeded jitter of ±0.025 cm keeps results deterministic while still breaking symmetry.
- **Measurement and output.**
  - Metrics: folds (outward crossings of the smoothed hem radius past its mean), hem flare, hem drop, maximum structural strain, minimum body clearance and residual speed.
  - Shaded SVG view: back half, then the body, then the front half, with Lambert shading.
  - OBJ export in centimetres with panel groups.
- **Storage.** The fabric is an optional `construction.fabric` field: validated, kept by pattern edits, grading and history. The simulation result is never saved.

## Results on a measured skirt

Skirt: waist 80 cm, hem 180 cm, slant 60 cm. Body: waist 76 cm, hip 100 cm, depth 20 cm.

| Fabric | Folds | Hem flare | Hem drop | Max stretch | Run time (Node) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chiffon | 15 | 0.60 | 2.25 cm | 3.3% | ~2.9 s |
| Cotton | 11 | 0.64 | 2.15 cm | 3.9% | ~2.7 s |
| Linen | 11 | 0.65 | 2.10 cm | 3.4% | ~2.6 s |
| Denim | 9 | 0.67 | 2.05 cm | 2.8% | ~2.5 s |

- **Body clearance** stayed at the 0.4 cm contact offset.
- **Hem drop** stays within the geometric bound: when the flare collapses, the slant hangs more vertically, so the hem can fall at most slant − rigid height ≈ 2.1 cm, plus the 0.5% warp tolerance.
- **Fabric trends** match expectations: lighter, softer fabric forms more, narrower folds and hangs closer to the body.
- **Not calibrated.** No measured drape coefficients are used, so these values are not calibrated against physical fabric tests.

## Verification (local, 2026-10-13)

- `npm run check` passes: **70** unit tests, 4 of them new in `tests/drape.test.ts`, plus lint and the build. The new tests cover:
  - an unstretched initial mesh from the patterns;
  - a fixed waist, no penetration, stretch below 5%, a settled state, the bounded hem drop and determinism;
  - chiffon forming more folds and less flare than denim;
  - the body proxy and assumed profile, fabric validation, and OBJ/SVG structure.
- Native Chromium: **all 77 cases pass**, 2 of them new in `tests/drape.spec.ts`.
  - First case: the disabled state before a pattern exists; a real worker simulation with 2,304 faces; a real OBJ with 2,400 vertices, the waist row at y = 0 and the hem below 50 cm; rotation that leaves the study unchanged.
  - The same case covers the fabric as an undoable saved edit with stale-result warnings, chiffon forming more folds than denim, and pattern edits keeping the fabric across a reload.
  - Second case: entered body measurements used and reported, cancellation leaving the study unchanged, clearance of at least 0.3 cm, and a 390 px layout.
- Screenshots of chiffon and denim drape were inspected.

## Not established

- Calibration against measured fabric properties or physical drape tests (for example, a drape coefficient).
- Friction, motion, collisions of the fabric with itself, thickness and a real avatar.
- Garments other than the two-panel skirt.
