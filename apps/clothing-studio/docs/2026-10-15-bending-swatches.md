## Dimensional model

This is a small-deflection Euler–Bernoulli cantilever, not a shell cloth solver. Both displacement and rotation at the first node are clamped. The last node receives a transverse force F; there is no distributed gravity load. Length L and width W are in metres. Directional bending rigidity D is N·m; strip flexural rigidity EI=D*W is N·m². The energy is `integral(EI*curvature²/2)dx - F*w(L)`.

Each equal element uses cubic Hermite displacement/rotation degrees of freedom and the standard4×4 beam stiffness matrix scaled by EI/h³. Fix the two clamp DOFs, solve the remaining at-most32 equations with partial-pivot elimination, then recompute energy and constrained/free forces from the original assembled matrix. Force and moment residuals are separate (N and N·m), never combined into a unitless error norm. JSON provides signed supportForceN/supportMomentNm in the positive-downward-deflection/rotation convention and explicit absolute clamp magnitudes; the UI labels those magnitudes as opposing the end load.

The independent continuum oracle is `w(x)=F*x²*(3L-x)/(6EI)` and `theta(x)=F*x*(2L-x)/(2EI)`. Tip values are `F*L³/(3EI)` and `F*L²/(2EI)`; stored energy is `F*tip/2`, reaction force magnitude F and clamp moment magnitude F*L. [MIT2.002 lecture2, David Parks,2004](https://www.ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-002-mechanics-and-materials-ii-spring-2004/bc25a56b5a91ad29ca5c7419616686f7_lec2.pdf), tip-loaded cantilever section, supplies the beam-theory reference. The conversion from the illustrative strip D to EI and the bounds below are our stated modeling decisions; no measured cloth coefficients are copied from the lecture.

Illustrative flexible: density0.18kg/m², warp D0.002N·m and weft D0.001N·m. Illustrative firm: density0.32kg/m², warp D0.02N·m and weft D0.01N·m. These are repository-authored numerical demonstrations, not material-library fabric specimens. Changing density alone changes mass, never D or static deflection. There is no link from these bending parameters to #266's tensile coefficients or the existing garment presets.
