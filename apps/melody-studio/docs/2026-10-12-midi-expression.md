# Optional expressive MIDI (#198)

The full DAW workflow needs volume automation to travel to external tools. Export MIDI with volume automation now adds7-bit CC11 expression for committed volumeCurve×volumeRamp while retaining ordinary Export MIDI byte-for-byte. CC7 continues to carry track volume; note timing/velocities, track programs and conductor tempo remain unchanged. The official controller table identifies CC7 channel volume and CC11 expression:https://midi.org/midi-1-0-control-change-messages (verified2026-10-12).

The exporter samples every30ticks(1/16beat at480PPQN), adding rounded curve/ramp breakpoint ticks and the common note-timeline endpoint. An upper bound includes all possible noteevents, metadata and breakpoints;60or120ticks(1/8or1/4beat) are chosen for larger graphs to fit the existing32768event limit. Equal consecutive CC11values are omitted. Expression precedes noteevents at shared ticks; muted parts receive no expression events. Validated detached input and strictboolean option prevent malformed publication. New pointer/keyboard guards protect every raw editor field before blur, and export creates no history/reference/project mutation.
